## 🎯 About Us

**Ingenium Society Club** is a student-driven engineering platform that channels students' interest in engineering into structured, sustainable, real-world projects. From identifying needs, to designing solutions, to building and testing them — members go through the complete engineering process, whether the outcome lives inside the school environment, on a competition stage, or as a portfolio piece.

We believe success isn't measured by whether every project ships — it's measured by the **documented engineering process and reflection** each project generates.

## 🚀 Mission

- Provide an official platform for students to build real engineering skills through real projects
- Increase the number of student-designed solutions actually implemented within the school
- Optimize facility and student-need improvements through structured engineering approaches
- Establish a clear, sustainable club structure that lasts beyond any one generation of members

## 🛠️ What We're Building
We are currently on planning stages after establishment.

## 🔁 Recurring Programs

- **The Workbench** — bi-weekly project pitch & build sessions, with university mentorship support
- **Trial & Iteration** — prototyping, testing, and iterating toward implementation or competition
- **Forge Outreach** — hands-on engineering workshops for partner elementary/junior high students
- **Lab Fundamentals** — equipment safety & usage training, required before using club lab resources

## 🏆 Annual Events

- **Ingenium Showcase** — our annual exhibition of finished and in-progress member projects
- **World Engineering Day** — our annual events of celebrating UNESCO's World Engineering Day
